# Remastered

Soldat's sounds remastered by **Coso**, licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0); the full text is in [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt).

Each sound in `sfx/` takes the place of the game's sound of the same name; the sounds
it hasn't are the game's own. It changes only how the game sounds, so it can be worn
with a mod of art.
